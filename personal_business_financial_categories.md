# Personal & Business Financial Category System

This Markdown file combines two category systems:

1. **Balance Sheet Categories** — what you own, what you owe, and your net worth/equity.
2. **Profit & Loss Categories** — income, expenses, and profit/loss over a period of time.

---

# Part 1: Balance Sheet Categories

A balance sheet shows financial position at a specific point in time.

```text
Assets = Liabilities + Equity / Net Worth
```

For personal finances, equity is usually called **net worth**.

```text
Personal Assets - Personal Liabilities = Personal Net Worth
```

For business finances:

```text
Business Assets - Business Liabilities = Business Equity
```

# Part 2: Profit & Loss Categories

A Profit & Loss report shows financial activity over a period of time.

```text
Income - Expenses = Net Income / Net Profit
```

For personal finances:

```text
Personal Income - Personal Expenses = Personal Surplus or Deficit
```

For business finances:

```text
Business Revenue - Business Expenses = Business Profit or Loss
```

# 8. Best Practice for Scaling

Do not create separate categories for every business, project, customer, or app feature.

Avoid messy categories like:

```text
Tax App AWS Hosting
QuickBooks Clone AWS Hosting
Banking Page AWS Hosting
AI Advisor AWS Hosting
```

Use clean categories instead:

```text
Expense → Software & Technology → Hosting → AWS
```

Then use tags/classes for extra tracking:

```text
Business: Tax App
Project: AI Advisor
Department: Engineering
Customer: Internal
Feature: Banking
```

This keeps your financial reports clean while still letting you filter by project, business, feature, customer, or department.

# 9. Simple Account Classification Rules

Use these rules when deciding where something belongs.

## Balance Sheet

Use the balance sheet when the item is:

- Something you own
- Money owed to you
- Money you owe
- A loan balance
- An asset balance
- Equity or net worth
- Inventory balance
- Accounts receivable
- Accounts payable
- Customer deposit
- Unearned revenue
- Owner contribution
- Owner draw

## Profit & Loss

Use the Profit & Loss report when the item is:

- Income earned
- Revenue from customers
- Cost of producing/delivering goods or services
- Rent expense
- Payroll expense
- Advertising expense
- Software expense
- Insurance expense
- Travel expense
- Food/dining expense
- Interest expense
- Depreciation expense
- Amortization expense

## Common Split Examples

### Loan Payment

```text
Interest portion → Profit & Loss
Principal portion → Balance Sheet
```

### Mortgage Payment

```text
Interest portion → Profit & Loss
Principal portion → Balance Sheet
Escrow portion → Balance Sheet or Expense, depending on setup
```

### Credit Card Payment

```text
Original purchases → Profit & Loss or Balance Sheet, depending on what was bought
Payment to credit card → Balance Sheet liability reduction
Interest/fees → Profit & Loss
```

### Buying Equipment

```text
Equipment purchase → Balance Sheet asset
Depreciation over time → Profit & Loss expense
```

### Customer Prepayment

```text
Customer deposit received → Balance Sheet liability
Revenue earned later → Profit & Loss income
```

### Inventory Purchase

```text
Inventory purchase → Balance Sheet asset
Inventory sold → Profit & Loss COGS
```
